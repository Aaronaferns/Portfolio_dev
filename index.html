Computer-Use Automation System

Sep – Oct 2026

An AI agent (Claude, driving a real browser through Playwright) learns a task in an old web ERP once. That run is compiled into a typed file a person reviews and signs off on, and from then on a plain interpreter replays it with no model in the loop, so repeat runs cost nothing in model calls. The task posts payments, so it is built to fail safe: a SQLite ledger records intent before every click and never posts a payment twice, a lost response goes to a person instead of being retried, the model never sees real credentials, and anything off the approved action list is blocked. An operator can take over the same live browser mid-run. Tested against Dolibarr with injected network faults, with Dolibarr's own API as the referee: 33 of 33 lookup runs and 5 of 5 payment cases passed, backed by 850+ automated tests.

ScoutVideo

2026

A voice agent you can talk to about driving footage. You speak, it answers about what is on screen or finds the moment you describe, and it stops talking about a quarter second after you cut in. A zero-temperature router sends frame, video, comparison and search questions straight to the right tool; everything else goes to Qwen3-8B with six tools it can call. Vision questions go to Cosmos-Reason2, which keeps frames out of the chat context. Scene search runs over a FalkorDB knowledge graph of nuScenes scenes (559 nodes, 1,790 edges) built straight from the raw dataset tables. Questions about whether the car is turning are answered from its recorded motion data, because a front camera can't see its own car turn.

Next-Item Recommendation (RePrior-Rec)

2026

Given a shopper's history, predict the first new item they will touch in the next week, ranking all 42,250 items in the catalog with no sampled shortcuts. I compared 18 models on 2.8 million RetailRocket events, from popularity counts and session kNN to SASRec, EASE and transformers with RL heads, each scored once on a held-out week with bootstrap error bars. My hybrid, RePrior-Rec, is a transformer that learns a correction to a simpler EASE recommender, with a copy head for repeat visits. It beat the strongest baseline by 14%, and an XGBoost reranker on top scored best overall. The breakdown taught me more than the headline: about 62% of the new model's score came from the 9% of shoppers returning to something they had already viewed, and swapping RL objectives moved results by less than the error bars.

LedgerBench

Oct 2026

A small benchmark for LLM agents, built so I could trust its scores. 40 ledger tasks, each gold answer computed twice by separate code, plus an LLM judge that grades one rubric item per call. Fake models went through first: a perfect answer had to score full marks, and empty, hedged or prompt-injection answers had to score zero. Scores cover pass@1, pass@k and pass^k with Wilson, bootstrap and task-clustered intervals, and a power analysis showed 40 tasks can only catch gaps of about 15 points. Claude Haiku and Sonnet both solved all 40, Haiku at under half the cost per solved task, so the next version needs harder tasks.

re-diffusion

2025 – 2026

A 254M-parameter latent Diffusion Transformer written from scratch: attention, AdaLN-Zero conditioning, patchify and the rectified-flow math by hand, with only the VAE and CLIP text encoder pretrained. Run on about 2 million LAION-Aesthetics images with DDP and DeepSpeed. Profiling showed the data pipeline was never the problem (757 images/s against a model using about 26) and hand-written fp32 attention was. Swapping in fused attention gave 2.2× throughput and half the peak memory.
